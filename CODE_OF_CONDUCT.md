# Code of conduct

Musico Online is a small, local-first music player. Contributions are welcome
from anyone who wants to make it better. The rules are short.

## Expected behavior

- Be direct about code, generous about people.
- Critique the change, not the contributor.
- Respect privacy: do not publish someone's personal information.
- Keep issues and pull requests on topic. Music talk belongs in Discussions.
- Assume good faith; ask before escalating.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language or imagery.
- Trolling, spam, or repeated off-topic posting.
- Publishing private information without permission.

## Scope

This applies in GitHub issues, pull requests, reviews, commit messages,
Discussions, and project chat such as `agentchat.md`.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report a problem privately to [@ARYAN007H](https://github.com/ARYAN007H) on
GitHub. Do not open a public issue about it. Reports are handled quietly.

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close threads, or block accounts.
